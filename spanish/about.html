Our mission

We were founded with one mission: to make real English accessible. We want every student, from the very first day, to speak, understand and use the language with confidence.

We believe a language is learned by using it. That is why every class is designed for students to take part, ask questions, make mistakes and try again in an atmosphere of trust.

Speak with courage.
Learn with purpose.

Our motto

English for real life

Leticia is a meeting point: travellers from many countries cross paths here, and Spanish, Portuguese and the languages of the region blend together. In a place like this, speaking English opens doors in tourism, in studies and at work.

Our goal is not to memorise rules, but to use English in real situations: introducing yourself, asking questions, following a conversation, writing a message or solving a problem.

Registration and institutional status

Kingsbridge English Academy is an institution founded and registered with the Chamber of Commerce under number 26385. Its work currently takes place online and through individual in-person classes, and it plans a physical campus of its own.

In operationOnline and at your doorThe academy operates online on a regular basis. It offers an interactive practice platform organised according to the Common European Framework of Reference for Languages (CEFR), a level test and learning resources for children and adults, accessible from any device. It also gives individual in-person classes, at home or in hotels in Leticia.
PlannedPhysical campus in LeticiaThe academy plans to open a physical campus of its own and is taking part in the SENA Fondo Emprender process to make it possible. Meanwhile, in-person classes are given individually, at home or in hotels, and partnerships with universities and schools are being pursued to offer group classes until the campus opens.

This sequence is deliberate. Consolidating a solid digital foundation first, with structured content and clear assessment criteria, allows the future campus to rest on a proven methodology rather than on improvisation. The academy will continue to expand what it offers in an orderly way.

Frequently asked questions

How do I get started?

You can message us on WhatsApp and tell us what you need, or leave your details in the pre-enrolment form. We will reply with information about the classes.

What ages are the classes for?

We have classes for children, teens and adults. We also offer support for school English classes.

Do I need to know English to start?

No. You can start from zero. If you do not know your level, take our free level test or write to us and we will guide you.

How much do the classes cost?

Message us on WhatsApp and we will send you the current pricing.

Is the academy registered?

Yes. We are registered with the Chamber of Commerce under No. 26385. The digital platform is already running, we give individual in-person classes, and the physical campus in Leticia is a future project.

Are there in-person classes?

Yes. We give individual in-person classes, at home or in hotels in Leticia. We do not have a physical campus of our own yet, and we are pursuing partnerships with universities and schools to offer group classes as well until the campus opens. Message us on WhatsApp to arrange a time and place.
