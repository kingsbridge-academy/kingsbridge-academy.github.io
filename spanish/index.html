Levels

What you will practise

◆

Pick the level that fits you. You can change it at any time.

I

A1 · Beginner

Ser and estar, articles, gender, the present tense, numbers and everyday questions.

II

A2 · Elementary

Preterite and imperfect, reflexive verbs, por and para, comparisons and object pronouns.

III

B1 · Intermediate

First steps with the subjunctive, commands, the future, the conditional and double pronouns.

IV

B2 · Upper intermediate

Hypothetical sentences, the passive se, time clauses, connectors and collocations.

V

C1 · Advanced

Subjunctive nuances, concessive clauses, the passive voice, idioms and word formation.

Questions

Frequently asked questions

◆
Do I need an account?

No. Choose your level, pick an exercise and type a nickname. Your progress is saved on your own device.

Which level should I choose?

Start with the level that feels comfortable. If the questions are too easy or too hard, tap “Change level” at any time.

Can I use it on my phone?

Yes. The page is designed to work on phones, tablets and computers.

Are the levels official?

The levels follow the CEFR scale (A1 to C1), used worldwide to describe language ability. This page is not affiliated with Instituto Cervantes.

Where is Kingsbridge English Academy?

In Leticia, Amazonas, Colombia. The academy is registered with the Chamber of Commerce (No. 26395). You can reach us on WhatsApp.
