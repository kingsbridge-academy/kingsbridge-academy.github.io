Phrase 1 / 8HolaHelloOH-lah

The “h” is silent in Spanish.

Phrase 2 / 8Buenos díasGood morningBWEH-nohs DEE-ahs

Use it until around noon, then switch to “buenas tardes”.

Phrase 3 / 8Muchas graciasThank you very muchMOO-chahs GRAH-see-ahs

The reply is “de nada”, which means “you’re welcome”.

Phrase 4 / 8¿Cómo estás?How are you?KOH-moh ehs-TAHS

With someone older, or in a formal setting, say “¿Cómo está usted?”.

Phrase 5 / 8Mucho gustoNice to meet youMOO-choh GOOS-toh

Say it when you are introduced to someone.

Phrase 6 / 8¿Dónde está el baño?Where is the bathroom?DOHN-deh ehs-TAH ehl BAH-nyoh

A handy phrase for any trip. Spanish opens questions with an upside-down “¿”.

Phrase 7 / 8PerdónExcuse mepehr-DOHN

Use it to get someone’s attention or to apologise. “Disculpe” is a polite alternative.

Phrase 8 / 8Por favorPleasepohr fah-VOHR

Add it to any request to sound polite.
